What to say on a first pass

  • What you love and will not give up.
  • What is wrong, old, or already fixed — in your words.
  • What a photo will not show.
  • What you think the house is worth, and why, without handing that number to a lead portal first.
  • Title, showing rules, and who is allowed to talk about this house.
